About us

Built on 9 years of running real ad budgets.

Hrid Digital Group was started by a paid media specialist who has spent more than nine years managing campaigns on Google, Meta, Amazon, LinkedIn and Local Services Ads.

Our story

After years of running campaigns for businesses of every size, one pattern kept repeating: companies were spending money on ads without knowing what they got back. Tracking was broken, reports were confusing, and nobody owned the results.

Hrid Digital Group exists to fix that. We set up tracking first, run your campaigns with the same care we would give our own money, and report in plain language tied to leads and sales.

Why "Hrid"? Hrid (हृद्) is Sanskrit for heart, the centre of something. Your business growth is at the centre of everything we do.

What we believe

Four rules we work by with every client.

Tracking before spending

No campaign goes live until conversions are measured correctly.

Plain-language reporting

You see leads, sales and cost per result, not vanity metrics.

Your accounts stay yours

Ad accounts, pixels and data are always owned by you.

One accountable team

Ads, social and tracking managed together, so nothing falls between agencies.
