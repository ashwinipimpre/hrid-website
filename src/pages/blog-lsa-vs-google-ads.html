Local Services Ads vs Google Search Ads: which one fits your business?

Local Services Ads · Hrid Digital Group

If you run a local service business, such as plumbing, cleaning, HVAC or legal services, Google offers two very different ways to appear at the top of search. Here is how they compare.

Local Services Ads: pay per lead

Local Services Ads (LSA) appear above regular search ads and show your business with ratings and, after you pass Google's screening, a trust badge such as "Google Guaranteed" or "Google Screened".

  • You pay for leads (calls and messages), not clicks.
  • Setup needs business verification, licences or insurance checks depending on your category, and background checks in some cases.
  • Availability depends on your country and business category, so check whether yours is supported.
  • You have less control: there are no keywords or ad copy to write.

Google Search Ads: pay per click

  • You choose keywords, write ads and send people to your own landing page.
  • Available in almost every country and industry.
  • You pay for clicks, so tracking and a good landing page matter a lot.
  • More control means more room to optimise, and more room to waste budget if managed poorly.

Which should you choose?

If LSA is available for your category, it is often the fastest way to get calls, because the badge and reviews build trust. Search Ads fill the gaps: services LSA does not cover, areas outside your LSA radius, and high-value keywords you want full control over.

Many local businesses get the best result by running both, together with an optimised Google Business Profile, so they appear in the LSA, ads and map results at the same time.
