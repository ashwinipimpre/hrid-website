Why conversion tracking comes before ad spend

Tracking · Hrid Digital Group

Ad platforms optimise towards whatever you tell them is a success. If tracking is missing or wrong, they optimise towards the wrong thing, and you have no reliable way to tell good campaigns from bad ones.

What goes wrong without it

  • Smart bidding strategies like Maximise Conversions have nothing accurate to learn from.
  • Duplicate tags count one lead twice, so results look better than they are.
  • Phone calls and WhatsApp clicks, often your best leads, are not counted at all.

Our pre-launch checklist

  1. Google Tag Manager installed once on every page, holding all your tags in one place.
  2. Google Analytics 4 with key events for leads, purchases and sign-ups.
  3. Google Ads conversions for forms, calls and purchases, with enhanced conversions turned on where possible.
  4. Meta Pixel and Conversions API, so Meta still receives events when browsers block tracking.
  5. LinkedIn Insight Tag for B2B campaigns.
  6. Call and WhatsApp tracking for click-to-call and click-to-chat buttons.
  7. A test submission on every form, checked in each platform before launch.

Check your own setup

Submit a test enquiry on your website, then check whether it appears in GA4 and in your ad platforms within a day. If it does not, or appears twice, your campaigns are learning from bad data.
