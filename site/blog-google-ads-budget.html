How much should a small business spend on Google Ads?

Google Ads · Hrid Digital Group

"How much should I spend?" is the first question almost every business owner asks. There is no single right number, but there is a simple way to work out a sensible starting budget for your business.

Start from the leads you need

Work backwards from your goal. Three numbers do most of the work:

  • Leads you want per month. For example, 30 enquiries.
  • Conversion rate. The share of website visitors who become a lead. Many service businesses see somewhere between 3% and 10%; use your own data if you have it.
  • Average cost per click (CPC). Google Keyword Planner shows estimated bids for your keywords and location.

The simple formula

Monthly budget = leads needed ÷ conversion rate × cost per click.

Example: you want 30 leads, your site converts 5% of visitors, and clicks cost about ₹40. You need 30 ÷ 0.05 = 600 clicks, and 600 × ₹40 = ₹24,000 a month.

Give it time to learn

The first 4 to 8 weeks are about learning which keywords, ads and audiences work. Results usually improve as poor search terms are excluded and budget moves to what converts. Judge the account on cost per lead over a few months, not the first week.

Three ways small budgets get wasted

  • Broad keywords with no negatives. Your ads show for searches that will never buy.
  • No conversion tracking. Google cannot optimise for leads it cannot see.
  • Sending traffic to the home page. A focused landing page usually converts better.
